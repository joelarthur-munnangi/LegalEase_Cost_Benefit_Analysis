How the analysis works

Development costs occur at month 0. Monthly operational costs and benefits remain constant from month 1. The scenario multiplier applies to all benefit categories, while costs stay fixed. The horizon is measured from launch.

Total cost = development + monthly operations × months.
Total benefits = monthly benefits × realization factor × months.
Net benefits = total benefits − total cost.
BCR = total benefits / total cost.
Payback = development / (realized monthly benefits − monthly operations), if monthly net cash flow is positive.

This is an undiscounted model, without taxes, inflation, growth, launch delay, or financing costs. Currency selection changes the display unit; it does not convert values. Revenue benefits should use contribution margin for a cash-based comparison. Time savings count only when they create measurable capacity or spending reductions.
